## What Aether sends, and what it keeps

Aether reads nothing about your computer to identify it. It does not read hardware identifiers, Windows' machine ID, your computer's name or your user name.

To check membership, Aether sends the developer's membership server:

- the AdventureQuest Worlds account name you sign in with;
- a random identifier that this copy of Aether made up for itself the first time it ran, which names nothing on your computer;
- the version of Aether you are running.

The server reads the guild shown on your public AdventureQuest Worlds character page. It keeps your account name, a scrambled (hashed) form of the random identifier, and when that copy last checked in, so it can confirm membership and notice an account being shared between many copies. Like any website, the server sees the network address a request comes from; it does not keep it.

Aether also asks GitHub whether a newer version has been released, and the game itself talks to Artix Entertainment's servers exactly as it would in a browser.

Aether's logs and crash reports stay on your computer. They are only ever seen by anyone else if you choose to send them.
